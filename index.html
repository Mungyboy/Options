Strategy Rules — Quick Reference
  • 1Open this tracker every morning before market open. All tickers are independent — trade each separately.
  • 2Enter only when all 5 conditions are ✓: price above cloud, Tenkan > Kijun, Chikou clear, ADX > 25, DI+ > DI−.
  • 3Buy ATM CALL, nearest expiry ~35 DTE (Friday). One contract = one trade unit.
  • 4Stop: Exit immediately if the option drops −50% from your entry price. No exceptions.
  • 5Trail stop (profit only): Once the stock closes above your entry price, trail at [highest close since entry − N×ADR14]. Exit next open if breached. Multipliers: SPY 4×, QQQ 3.5×, MSFT/GLD 1.5×, NVDA 2×, SMH 2.5×, AMD 3×. Below entry, only the −50% option hard stop applies.
  • 6Time exit: Close the trade when ≤ 2 trading days remain on the option, regardless of P&L.
  • 7No signal = no trade. You can hold an existing position while signal stays active.
